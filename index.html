Clarity starts with good data.

DataNest Analytics is a concept studio built around one idea: analysis is only useful when people can follow it. We keep the work simple, documented, and easy to explain.

Organize raw data

Raw files are rarely ready to use. We sort out structure, formats, and gaps first.

Find patterns

We look at how the data behaves over time, across groups, and at the edges.

Present useful metrics

We choose measures that match the question being asked, and define each one plainly.

Keep it understandable

Findings come with the reasoning behind them, in language non-specialists can read.

Services

Four connected steps, from messy source data to clear reporting.

Data Cleaning & Preparation

Exploratory Data Analysis

SQL & Business Reporting

Our process

  1. Understand the question

    Agree on what needs answering before touching the data.

  2. Prepare and validate the data

    Clean, check, and document what the data does and does not contain.

  3. Analyze patterns and key metrics

    Explore trends and calculate the measures that matter.

  4. Present findings clearly

    Share results in charts and plain language.
